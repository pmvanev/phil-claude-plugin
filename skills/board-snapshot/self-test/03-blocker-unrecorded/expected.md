## What must happen

The first blocked card names what it waits on, taken from its `## Chain` line. The second is rendered as
blocked with its blocker stated to be **unrecorded**.

## What must NOT happen

Nothing is inferred. Not from the title, which mentions another card; not from the sibling card that
discusses the dependency in prose. Both are available, both are plausible, and both would produce a
sentence a later reader cannot tell from a recorded one.

The second card is not omitted. A blocked card missing from the blocked section is the worst available
outcome — it is the section a reader trusts most.

## Why this fixture exists

Issue #34 named this as open: the forge records that a card is blocked and the reason lives in prose.
Saying "unrecorded" is the honest answer, and it is also a finding the reader can act on.
