# Expected — BS-SELFTEST-09

`DECLARED`, written as given, with the disagreement recorded beside it.

## What must happen

The declaration is written verbatim: **single-valued**. Beside it, the observation — that N issues
currently carry more than one member, named by number.

**Recorded, not resolved.** The human is the authority on a fact no forge records; the observation is
what a later reader needs to understand why the answer looked odd, and possibly to discover that the
board has drifted from the decision rather than the decision being wrong.

## What must NOT happen

No refusal. No re-asking — the answer was unambiguous, and re-asking an answered question reads as
disbelief. No adjustment toward what the labels suggest. And no silent dropping of the observation.

## Why this fixture exists

This is the case that decides whether the elicitation is real. If the tool overrides a declaration it
disagrees with, then it was never asking — it was seeking confirmation of an inference it had already
made, which is precisely what [D6] forbids and what would mint the declaration rule 4 exists to read.

The inverse also matters, and is handled the same way: declaring a family multi-valued when nothing on
the board carries two is equally a disagreement, and is equally written as given.

Note that this repo's own real answers do **not** exercise this path — both agreed with observed use.
It is covered by unit test rather than by dogfood, and that is recorded in slice 03's brief.
