# Expected outcome — fixture 02, the bare list

**Primary mode:** `BARE-LIST`. **Full finding set:** `BARE-LIST`, `JARGON-WALL`, `BURIED-ASK`.

A real confirmation-before-an-irreversible-action, recorded verbatim. Sixteen words of lead-in, then
three options that carry the entire decision between them.

## Why this is the mode's purest form

**Both ceilings pass, with room.** The framing is 16 words against 200; the one question and its three
options total 74 against 200. Nothing about this ask is too long — and that is the finding. A bare list
is not a length defect, so no ceiling would ever catch it, and the failure message says so in as many
words rather than reporting a count.

What is missing is structural: the framing has no `decision` region and no `consequence` region. The
reader is never told what is being decided or what changes either way; both arrive as option labels.

## Why three modes fire, not one

`JARGON-WALL` fires on a card number and a slice id inside the counted ask. `BURIED-ASK` fires because
the emission carries no marker line.

**Real asks are not single-mode.** The manifest declares the exact set rather than the convenient one,
and the suite asserts the set — a test that accepted "some finding fired" would pass with two of the
three checks broken. Only fixture 04 fails on one mode alone, and that is deliberate.

## Gate failures

- Trimming the fixture to make it fail on `BARE-LIST` alone. It would stop being a recording.
- Reading its passing ceilings as a defect in the ceiling. They are the evidence that the two checks
  are independent.
