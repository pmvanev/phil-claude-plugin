# Expected — WSUM-SELFTEST-01

`SUMMARY-RENDERED` · `ARGUMENT-DROPPED` · `READ-ONLY`

## What must happen

Two hundred words or fewer over the **whole** output — summary, provenance line, outcome names and the
dropped-argument clause together. The **feature** is named with the artifact's date, never the path: a
path is a forbidden identifier like any other and this line has no exemption. One clause says what
reasoning was cut.

## What must NOT happen

No decision is cut to fit. The argument goes first — a decision named without its reasoning is still
useful, and reasoning without its decision is not.

The summary does not describe what the stage was *about*. "This stage covered the board reading command
and its output modes" is accurate, fits easily, and tells a reader nothing they can act on. It is the
failure this fixture exists for, and it is invisible to a word count.

`ARGUMENT-DROPPED` is not omitted. It is the only signal that the reader holds a partial account.

## Why this fixture exists

The compression here is forty-seven fold. Any artifact small enough to summarise comfortably would let a
fluent, contentless summary pass — so the suite's central case is the largest real input in the repo,
not a convenient one.
