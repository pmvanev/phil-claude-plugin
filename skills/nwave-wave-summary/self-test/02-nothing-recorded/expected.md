# Expected — WSUM-SELFTEST-02

`NOTHING-RECORDED` · `READ-ONLY`

## What must happen

One sentence: what was looked for, what was found. The artifact is named.

## What must NOT happen

Nothing is summarised and nothing is inferred — not from the headings, not from the feature's name, not
from its slice briefs or commits, not from a sibling feature. Every one of those is available and every
one would produce a sentence a later reader cannot distinguish from a real finding.

The absence is not described at length. A paragraph about emptiness is padding.

## Why this fixture exists

The command creates a new way to be wrong. A confident summary of a stage that decided nothing reads
exactly like a summary of one that decided something, and the reader has no way to tell — which is worse
than the afternoon's read it replaced.
