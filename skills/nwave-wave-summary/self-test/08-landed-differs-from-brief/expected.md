## What must happen

Two hundred words naming what landed beyond the brief — the repo-wide guard and the sibling change — and

## What must NOT happen

The brief is not restated shorter. That is the version the card doubted, and it is what this mode has to
beat to justify existing.

The comparison is not by file path, and the `path_diff_shaped` counterexample is exactly what the
mechanical version emits: a file count dressed as a sentence, clean by every rule and worth nothing.

The brief is not restated — `brief_restated` is the second counterexample and the version the card
predicted.

Neither is caught by any pattern. **Measured on three real slices: a path diff produced 48 differences
of which roughly five were real**, because briefs describe work in prose — *"two fixtures in the existing
suite"* names no paths and plainly anticipates them. A mechanical version of this comparison is worse
than none.

The added work is not judged. Whether a repo-wide guard belonged in that slice is a real question and
another command's.

## Why this fixture exists

It is the mode's justification, and it was measured before the mode was built rather than assumed. Two of
three sampled slices landed substantive work their briefs never described. Had it been zero, the card's
doubt was right and this half should have been dropped.
