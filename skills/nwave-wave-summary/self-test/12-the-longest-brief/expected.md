# Expected — WSUM-SELFTEST-12

`SUMMARY-RENDERED` · `READ-ONLY`

## What must happen

Two hundred words or fewer over an input of 3,670 words plus the commits that shipped it. What landed is
named; the brief is the input and does not get to consume the budget.

## What must NOT happen

The brief is not summarised at the landed view's expense. On a long brief the pull is to spend the
ceiling restating intent and arrive at the outcome with nothing left — which produces exactly the shorter
brief this mode exists not to produce.

## Why this fixture exists

The stage mode had a largest-artifact fixture from its first commit; the slice mode shipped without one,
and no slice fixture recorded a word count, so the driver could not see the gap. This closes it at the
real maximum rather than a convenient one.
