## What must happen

The two undelivered items are named, individually. The summary does not say the slice shipped its brief.

## What must NOT happen

Only-what-landed is not reported: a list of delivered work reads as a complete one, and the reader has no
way to see what is missing.

The gap is not aggregated. "Two items outstanding" is a count, and a count is the thing this mode exists
to replace.

Nothing is inferred about *why*. Descoped, forgotten and deferred are three different facts and no
artifact here distinguishes them.

## Why this fixture exists

Naming what a brief promised and did not deliver is this mode's headline value — and this fixture was in
slice 02's own IN scope and did not ship. **A mode for finding undelivered promises, whose own brief
carried an undelivered promise, found by review rather than by its suite.** That is the fixture's subject
demonstrating itself, and it is why the omission is recorded in the delta rather than quietly filled.
