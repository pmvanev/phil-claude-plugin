# Expected — 37 (a habit is not a fact)

**Pins:** slice-07 AC2 and AC3 — the two evidence tiers that license nothing.

**Expected:** `SURFACE-CANDIDATE` only. The set is **reported**, and **no consolidation is offered**:

```
#51, #52, #53 may be slices of one feature -- their titles say 'billing slice NN'.
Not offering to consolidate: the naming is the only tie.
  no parent edge  ·  no body names a docs/feature/ directory
The shared milestone 'Q3 billing' is not evidence -- a milestone is a goal and holds
unrelated work by design.
Confirm they are one feature and I will offer it.
```

**Why the line is drawn here.** A `slice NN` title is a **convention someone followed**, and conventions are
followed inconsistently and borrowed across features. Consolidating on it closes real cards on the strength of
a naming habit. The parent edge and the artifact path are different in kind: the forge and the repository
*assert* the relationship, and neither can be a coincidence of wording.

**The milestone is the trap, and it is the strongest-*feeling* signal here.** Three cards, one goal, sequential
names — it reads conclusive. But `phil:issue-board` makes a milestone a **goal**, deliberately holding work
that is unrelated except in purpose. Treating it as structural evidence would consolidate every goal on the
board into one card.

**Gate failures:**

- Offering the consolidation. The defect this fixture exists for.
- Counting the milestone toward the evidence, even as a tiebreaker.
- Reporting nothing because the evidence is weak. The set is worth a human's eye; the *write* is what is
  withheld.
- Inferring the feature id from the titles and claiming the bodies name it.
