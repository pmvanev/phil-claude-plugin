# Expected outcome — fixture 43 (the container is a story card, never a milestone)

**Pins:** *features of one story, carded separately*, and its supersession of *ungrouped effort* —
*"offer only this one. Offering both invites a goal filed around work that needs a different kind of
container."*

**Expected:** one set-level finding naming all four cards, with the shared `Story: chat-everywhere`
declaration quoted as evidence. Offered resolution: **consolidate into one story card** whose feature
roster holds them as rows. **No milestone is offered.**

**Both classes fire and only one is offered.** Nothing on the forge groups these cards, so *ungrouped
effort* is genuinely true. But its resolution proposes a goal, and **a goal holds cards while a story
holds feature directories** — filing a milestone here groups them on the board while leaving the thing
that should be one card as four. It looks like a resolution and resolves nothing, which is worse than no
offer at all.

**This is the decomposed-feature supersession one level up**, and deliberately so: same shape, same
reason, same ranking of evidence. Recognising it as the same pattern is what kept it from being reasoned
out afresh and reaching a different answer.

**The evidence must be confirmed pushed.** A `Story:` line at column 0 in each delta **on the default
branch** — `git ls-tree`, not the working tree — licenses the offer; a shared title prefix or a common
milestone licenses a report only. A slug that exists only in someone's checkout is invisible to every
other reader, and consolidating on it builds a story card whose membership nobody else can verify.

**Consolidation is irreversible, so it is offered one candidate at a time.** Closing a feature card
discards its comments and history, and the rollup hazard applies unchanged: **un-parent before closing**,
or the parent renders complete while the work continues.

**Gate failures:**

- Offering a milestone. The defect, stated.
- Offering both a milestone and a story card. The reader picks one and half the board ends up in each
  convention.
- Reporting nothing because each card is individually well-formed. They are; the defect is between them.
- Offering on a shared title prefix alone, with no `Story:` declaration.
- Offering on declarations found only in the working tree.
- Offering the four as a group merge in one step. Irreversible operations go one at a time.
- Reporting them as a *decomposed feature*. Its members are slices; these are whole features.
