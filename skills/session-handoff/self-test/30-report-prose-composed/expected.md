# Expected — 30 (the standard reaches the report, and stops at the quote)

**Pins:** issue #43. Fixture 27's counterpart one layer out. 27 draws the line inside the *snapshot* —
the why and the next action composed, a frame's own words verbatim. This one draws it inside the
**report**.

**Expected decision:** `RECONSTRUCT` + `ASK-OWNER`, and **no board outcome**: with no snapshot there is
no recorded next action to compare, and the exemption is the criterion rather than the shape.

**No candidate prose is supplied, deliberately.** Composing these sentences is the act under test. A
fixture offering two wordings would test selection instead, which is passed by picking the shorter
string — the reading of concision this standard refuses.

## Composed, and therefore held to `rules/writing.md`

- The clause labelling the briefing **reconstructed, not recorded**.
- The sentence saying the *why* is unavailable because nothing derives it.
- The `ASK-OWNER` sentence — the owner is unknown, and the absence of a wave label is not permission.
- The sentence naming what the position was reconstructed *from*.

Active voice, positive form, definite and concrete language, the emphatic word last. **None of these is
invisible to a word count, and none of them is a word count**: this output sits far inside 300 words, so
the ceiling does nothing here and the standard still applies in full. That separation is the point of
the fixture.

## Quoted, and passed through untouched

**The commit subject `git log` returns**, verbatim — including its length, its semicolon and its version
bump. Also the branch name, the wave read from the artifacts, every outcome code, every commit hash and
every timestamp.

**Not a card title.** The board triple is the only place this skill prints one, and `RECONSTRUCT`
reports no board outcome — so on this path there is no card title to quote or to tighten. An earlier
draft of this fixture built its quoted half on one, which made its central gate unreachable: the run
would have emitted nothing to judge and passed by doing nothing.

## Gate failures

- **Tightening or summarising the quoted commit subject.** It is somebody else's words, and judging a
  human's prose is the taste this family refuses. A shorter subject in the output than in `git log` is
  the failure.
- **Reading the ceiling as the standard.** A run that composes flatly and defends it on length has
  confused a bound with a craft; concision is one of eleven principles of composition and the other ten do not
  yield to a short output.
- **Reading the standard as the ceiling.** Editing this report for brevity when it is already well
  under 300 words is the same confusion inverted.
- **Presenting position as a full briefing.** Wave, slice and branch are derivable and present; the why
  is neither, and a reconstruction that does not say so overstates what it knows.
- **Reporting `BOARD-AGREES`, `BOARD-DIVERGES` or `BOARD-UNREADABLE`.** All three are unproduceable
  here, and the last is false where the board reads perfectly.
- **Running the owning command**, or naming one from no label.
- **Claiming terminal-only output is exempt.** The discriminator is who composed the words, never where
  they sit.
