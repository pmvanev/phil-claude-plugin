# Convergence of the force curves

We compared the force on the proton across four k-point grids in the 32-atom lithium supercell. The
three larger grids produced curves that agree closely with each other, and the smallest grid produced
a curve that oscillates and never settles. Because the stopping power comes from a linear fit to the
work curve, the oscillation largely cancels, and every grid gives nearly the same stopping power.

The gauge correction holds the electron count constant throughout the simulation. It changes the
force curves by less than four percent and the stopping power by less than two, while doubling the
computational time or worse.
