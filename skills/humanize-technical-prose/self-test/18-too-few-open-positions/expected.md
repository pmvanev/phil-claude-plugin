# Expected — HTP-SELFTEST-18

`WROTE` · `SLIPS-SHORT` · `SLIPS-UNMIRRORED` · `SLIPS-WARNING`

## What must happen

- Exactly one slip is added, in *The upgrade takes a few minutes on a small install*, and it touches
  neither *a few* nor *small*. The script's slips are taken in order, and the first, a number
  agreement, fits there.
- `SLIPS-SHORT` says how many of 3 were added, and that the draft has no more open positions.

## What must NOT happen

- No slip lands in a command, a variable, a path, a version or the table.
- No slip lands in a sentence built around code.
- No sentence takes two slips, and no sentence is added to make room.

## Why this fixture exists

The count is a ceiling the draft may not meet. Meeting it anyway would push a slip into a command or
a claim, where a slip costs the reader most.
