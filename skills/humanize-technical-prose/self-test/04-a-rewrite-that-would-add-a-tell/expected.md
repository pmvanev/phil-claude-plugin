# Expected — HTP-SELFTEST-04

`WROTE` · `VOICE-SKIPPED` · `TELLS-LEFT`

## What must happen

- The first sentence becomes a plain statement, such as *The order service keeps writes strongly
  consistent.* The participle is cut: it names a motive the draft gives no evidence for.
- The re-check over that sentence finds no tell.
- *Experts argue that…* stays as written. It is listed under `TELLS-LEFT` as phantom attribution that
  needs a source the draft does not hold.
- The report's `TELLS:` line ends `re-check: 1 left`, and the one left is the attribution.

## What must NOT happen

- No tell survives in the first sentence, and none replaces the cut one. *This reflects the team's
  commitment to correctness* is a tell in its own right.
- No expert, paper or study is named.
- The attribution's claim is not cut to clear the tell. Cutting it would remove what the author said.

## Why this fixture exists

The re-check stops one tell being swapped for another. The needs-a-fact rule stops a citation being
invented to clear a tell.
